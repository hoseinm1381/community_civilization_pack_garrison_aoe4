# 0.7.6 reference and anatomy notes

New meshes and textures are generated by the release-owned authoring recipe. Official assets supply skeleton bind records, attachment contracts, scale comparisons and motion ownership; their visible geometry is retained hidden for comparison, not included in the exported new bodies.

## Visual references

- Water buffalo shape reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Water_buffaloes_of_Vietnam and https://vinalica.com.vn/tinh-trau-noi. Broad barrel, low forehead/muzzle, crescent swept horns, split hooves, horizontal ears, sparse tail tuft. Photographs are reference only; no image pixels copied into textures.
- Vietnamese clothing reference: the historical uniform illustration reproduced at https://www.pinterest.com/pin/1056797868812844181/ supports broad tunic/headwear silhouettes only. Its Nguyen period is later than the game setting. These designs are a stylized Dai Viet progression, not a claim of exact archaeological reconstruction.
- The user-approved progression governs rattan, cloth and selective metal reinforcement. No modern military helmet or firearm reference is used.

## Proportions and interfaces

- Buffalo: shoulder approximately 1.7 m in model coordinates, low head, barrel about 1.1 m wide, horn span about 1.7 m. Original 45-bone cow bind hierarchy preserved; new hide joined across torso/neck/leg intersections in revision r3. Hooves sit at ground level and horns seat into the forehead.
- Skirmishers: native 48-bone A-pose, feet near z=0, shoulders near z=1.52, head near z=1.7. New torso, trousers, sleeves, skin, headwear and equipment are generated around those measured anchors.
- Held javelin: grip origin z=0, original -0.8 to +2.33 m axial envelope. Projectile: forward +Y, 0.545 length conversion and +0.085 offset matching the separate native projectile envelope. Weapon and projectile share the same generation recipe and age-specific blade.
- Budgets and exact official triangle counts are in `output/release_076/budgets.json`; actual export LOD counts are recorded per revision. Every model has a 512x512 authored atlas and explicit normal, roughness, metalness and player-colour mask inputs.

The broad reference images are not redistributed. Native visual acceptance remains a separate user gate.
