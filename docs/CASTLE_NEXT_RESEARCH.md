# Castle asset research and interpretation

Approved 2026-10-17. This is asset preparation, without active source integration,
version changes, package creation, installation or native-match acceptance.

## Historical basis

- Thăng Long was established under the Lý dynasty in the eleventh century. Its
  archaeology supports a medieval northern Đại Việt direction, while UNESCO
  cautions that standing architecture has later alterations. The Citadel asset is
  a compact game interpretation, not a literal reconstruction of a surviving gate:
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1328/
- The National Museum documents terracotta building material and Lý–Trần–Lê
  decorative vocabulary: brick, roof tiles, bodhi-leaf ornaments, dragons,
  phoenixes and lotus patterns. Use restrained, silhouette-readable examples:
  https://baotanglichsu.vn/vi/Articles/3101/69653/vat-lieu-kien-truc-bang-djat-nung-tai-di-tich-hoang-thanh-thang-long.html
- Lam Sơn/Lũng Nhai supplies resistance context. The timber/bamboo headquarters
  and its functional compound layout are original art, not an archaeologically
  documented military headquarters plan:
  https://baotanglichsuquocgia.vn/vi/Articles/3091/14734/hoi-thao-khoa-hoc-hoi-the-lung-nhai-trong-khoi-nghia-lam-son.html
- The Trúc Lâm tradition belongs to the thirteenth/fourteenth-century Đại Việt
  setting. Monastery halls/courts/shrines use this direction without claiming
  that modern rebuilt temples reproduce medieval form exactly:
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1732/

## Art constraints and ownership

The two landmarks are scratch compounds with separate recognizable ruined forms.
Ordinary buildings use actual Chinese monastery age03/04, siege_workshop age03/04
and keep age03 native donors. Monastery visible redesign targets 65–80%, siege
30–40%, keep 25–35%; percentages are qualitative visual judgments, never mesh-area
measurements. Decorative overhang is approved; placement and functional markers
remain unchanged. Exact native attack/production access requires later game tests.

Editable sources, exports and recipes use `art/*/castle_next` on E:. Scripts use
`tools/castle_next`; large receipts, donor inputs, isolated projects and readbacks
use `output/castle_next` on the verified F: junction. The native cook helper keeps
short copies under the project's external `c/ccnext-*` namespace. These inputs
and failed diagnostic logs are retained; no release cleanup is authorized here.

The five stock remodels retain their material/owner-mask resources. The installed
Garrison static building adoption route exposes albedo/normal/roughness/metalness
but does not declare a dynamic owner-pattern input. New landmark pattern source
must therefore be retained and its exact native material support qualified;
neither a baked colour nor retained RGO/marker data is proof of dynamic colour.
