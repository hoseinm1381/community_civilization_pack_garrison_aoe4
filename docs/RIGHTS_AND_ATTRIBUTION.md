# Rights and attribution

Preserve existing credits **hosein81 and chilly5** and the credits/provenance already
present in the mod. Stock donors remain Age of Empires IV material; third-party tools,
libraries, models and provider-generated recordings can have separate terms.

This handoff does not invent a new license or claim that all historical assets have
the same redistribution rights. The maintainer should choose and document the mod's
contribution/distribution license with the relevant permissions before inviting
unrestricted reuse. Retain donor/source/provider attribution and existing notices.

Installed commercial editors, Garrison binaries, Python/CUDA environments and model
weights are not included as mod source. Contributor dependencies are installed
separately. Existing completed audio and project-owned inputs retain their original
provenance; no new paid requests or credential sharing is needed to work on them.
