## Consumer and chronology checklist

- Resolve actual canonical training/start/upgrade owners, not only files named `vietnam`. Source reachability is not a fresh-match availability test.
- Trace EBPS `speech_ext`, SBPS `squad_speech_ext`, base attachments and every active age/gender/appearance attachment's `attachment_speaker_code`. Shared original lists need private ownership before any later changes.
- Keep appearances, gameplay, equipment, genders, probabilities and animation bindings intact. Do not globally mute speech to hide overlap.
- Identify which human speaker responds for rider/crew units. Keep elephant/horse calls, hull/engine effects, weapons and impacts outside this dialogue replacement.
- Bank A1–A4 is a creative chronology. Blueprint tier, current player age, training age and native `cLowest/cLow/cMed/cHigh/cNone` are different facts. Their exact routing remains unqualified.
- A decoded profile may contain fishing, repair, transport and trade contexts even when its current consumer has only one of those capabilities. Never grant a ship gameplay because its speech profile has a word for it.
- `moveGC`, `moveSU`, `attackMoveGC`, `selectGC`, `acknowledgeGMR` and similar names are preserved exactly. Their suffixes are not expanded into guessed engine contracts.
