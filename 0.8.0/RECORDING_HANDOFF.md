# Recording handoff — draft scripts, not a recording order

## What is ready

The complete creative script bank has four chronological banks, role-specific actor identities, six phrases per semantic pool and three directed takes per phrase. It includes original nonverbal effort directions. Exact generated totals live in `SCRIPT_TOTALS.json`; do not quote an old count after editing source texts.

`VOICE_SCRIPTS.md` is the readable full bank; `VOICE_SCRIPTS.json` additionally includes per-line source IDs, confidence, modern tone-category breakdowns, reuse groups and activation conditions. `SAMPLE_LINES.md` is a short navigation preview, not the complete script.

## How to interpret totals

The expanded row/take totals are **research capacity**, including dormant ages, conditional contexts and mutually exclusive alternative pools. They are not the number of unique sentences and not an approved invoice for all recordings. `distinct_dialogue_texts` counts unique Vietnamese strings. Reusing a sentence does not create new wording; changing actor creates a different performance identity.

Before recording, choose each actual consumer's age policy and one pool per event. Disable unavailable actions, determine whether unchanged texts can share the same approved pronunciation across ages, and decide which efforts can be deliberately reused. Keep an explicit alias ledger. Do not record early-age gun or cannon banks simply because the dossier provides dormant research rows.

The native profile crosswalk preserves delivery/chronology labels, but those are not yet mapped. T1/T2/T3 are three performances of a phrase, **not** normal/high/stealth. If the final route needs separate delivery bands, they need separate directions, recordings and totals after qualification. Undecoded native profiles also require later inventory before any claim of complete native-slot replacement.

## Recording IDs and filenames

Stable phrase ID example: `VN070_ARCHER_A3_BOW_L01`. Record takes as `VN070_ARCHER_A3_BOW_L01_T1.wav`, `_T2.wav`, `_T3.wav`. Effort IDs use `E01` etc. Preserve exact ID case and UTF-8 metadata; filenames themselves use ASCII. Do not rename original source IDs after editorial revision; add a revision field/session folder such as `r02` and retain earlier masters.

Record one actor ID per session sheet. Capture the exact Vietnamese text, English intent, age target, context, take direction, native editor approval, historical reviewer approval, performer, rights/consent, dates, source master filename/hash and any retake note. This folder contains **zero** recorded audio files and does not nominate a paid service or clone a person's voice.

## Review gates before production

1. A native Vietnamese editor checks every line for idiom, meaning, address and task fit. No such review has happened yet.
2. A historical-language specialist checks lexical dates, proposed older forms, regional assumptions and pronunciation. Early whole-sentence IPA remains unresolved rather than fabricated.
3. A source/integration reviewer resolves inherited actors, age requirements, gender branches, all native speech consumers and event observability.
4. A small approved audition establishes actor continuity, tone intelligibility and ordinary/urgent/concealed performance where applicable.
5. Then create a final session manifest with only approved active rows, intended aliases and new-recording totals.

## Listening and preservation requirements

Listen for correct word/meaning/tones, natural Northern delivery, stable identity across age targets, real take differences and absence of clipping or unwanted room noise. Do not “fix” pronunciation by pitching a waveform. Nonverbal efforts are human-only; protect performers from forced throat strain and keep weapon/animal/mechanical effects separate.

Later game validation must prove correct age, role, event, gender, ownership and one-at-a-time selection, plus no original/custom overlap. A readable JSON, generated count, encoded WEM, successful cook, backend playback acknowledgement or package import does not prove any of those audible results.

Keep this dossier and preservation receipts as the preparation baseline. Future recording/integration work belongs to a separately authorized stage; none has started here.
