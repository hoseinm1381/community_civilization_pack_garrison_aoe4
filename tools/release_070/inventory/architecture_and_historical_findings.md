## Historical reference basis for blockouts

- **Thăng Long royal garden/elephant facility:** official heritage article [Hồ sen trong Hoàng thành Thăng Long qua tư liệu lịch sử và khảo cổ học](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/ho-sen-trong-hoang-thanh-thang-long-qua-tu-lieu-lich-su-va-khao-co-hoc/) documents royal gardens and lotus ponds through historical/archaeological evidence, including excavated pond remains and royal garden references. The reported excavated area is 6,720 m² (roughly 48 m east-west by 140 m north-south), with lotus remains; it supports an elongated pond/garden composition, not a game footprint. Historical sources mention rare animals and elephant handling in the wider royal compound, but this inventory found no measured elephant-stable plan or reliable building footprint. Show an elephant facility as an explicitly interpretive yard/stable element, not as an excavated structure.
- **Cannon foundry:** official Thăng Long heritage overview [Central Sector of the Imperial Citadel of Thăng Long – Hanoi](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/khu-trung-tam-hoang-thanh-thang-long-bao-vat-quoc-gia-tai-di-san-the-gioi-nam-2022/) identifies 17th-century Lê Trung Hưng cannon evidence. It does not document a recovered foundry building plan. Thus a royal cannon foundry should be labeled a reconstruction inspired by period cannon production, with no claim that its architecture is archaeologically attested.
- **Kính Thiên palace wonder:** official page [Điện Kính Thiên](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/dien-kinh-thien/) gives a 57 m × 41.5 m podium, 2.3 m high, with 10 stone steps in three aisles and four dragon pairs, and describes a Nhị-plan timber building with two tiers/eight roofs and curved eaves. The superstructure is lost; illustrations are reconstructions. Use the surviving stone stair/dragon platform as the strongest evidence and mark roof massing as interpretive. The official page provides image references including [dienkinhthien.jpg](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/dienkinhthien.jpg) and [dienkinhthien11.jpg](https://hoangthanhthanglong.vn/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/dienkinhthien11.jpg).
- **Văn Miếu–Quốc Tử Giám University:** [Vietnam Tourism’s Temple of Literature reference](https://vietnam.travel/node/1463) supports the 1070/1076 foundation, formal five-courtyard sequence, garden, and doctoral stele/turtle imagery. Official site history [site history](https://vanmieu.gov.vn/vi/site-history) describes the Lê Thái Học school complex (lecture hall, Minh Luân, dormitories, woodblock repository) while the current Thái Học buildings are 1999–2000 reconstructions; original buildings were destroyed. A maximum 50% visual remix can reasonably use a court-and-gate sequence, lecture hall, dormitory wings, and steles, but that percentage is an art target, not measured native geometry. Exclude the Khuê Văn pavilion: official [architecture page](https://www.vanmieu.gov.vn/vi/visit/architecture/khue-van-pavilion) dates it to 1805 and it does not fit the requested 15th–17th century grounding.

The machine-readable JSON records the references, baseline hashes, selector/footprint confidence, and unknowns. No geometry, gameplay, Blender scene, native cook, or source files were changed by this inventory.
