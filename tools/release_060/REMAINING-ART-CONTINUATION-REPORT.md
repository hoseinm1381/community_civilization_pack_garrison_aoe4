# Release 0.6.0 remaining unit art continuation — 2026-10-18

This report records editable source and material handoffs. It does **not** claim in-match appearance. `tools/verify_workspace.py --storage-only` and `common.guard()` passed before writes. No active gameplay source, previous release art, native cook, or final archive was changed by this art task.

## Exact three-LOD humanoid source

`remaining_roster_art_060.py` exported MAA III/IV male and female, Chinese repeater-crossbow IV, Chinese monk IV, and newly reached inherited Chinese monk III. Each receipt under `output/release_060/qualification/remaining_roster/<family>/<revision>/export.json` pins the exact decoded donor, editable `.blend`, three weighted LOD FBX, albedo, action and authored snapshot. Monk III is `monk3/v2` (an interrupted partial v1 is retained). Male/female MAA and repeater use v1; monk IV corrected source uses v2. The pipeline owns native geometry qualification and may reject or request a new revision. Attached staff, bows and spear models remain separate native resources.

Archer IV `v5c` corrects only four non-unit source corner normals; female villager III `v3` and monk IV `v2` each correct one. Earlier approved variants and albedos are preserved. The geometry pipeline has the exact correction receipts.

## Mounted bodies, traders and combat elephants

Twelve new armor, light, and male/female scout variants plus inherited trader II/III/IV are in `art/authoring/release_060/remaining_roster/mounted` with exact stock donor manifests, independent per-native-material albedos, and editable skinned cloth/tack details. Only the compatible stock-geometry/albedo path is qualified for root's material cook; the four extra coat/armor/blanket/tack source parts per model are **not** in that fallback. Every variant has separate stock-geometry fallback game and close renders under `output/release_060/qualification/remaining_roster/mounted/<key>/<revision>/fallback-review`. Horseman II v4 uses independently verified rider `single_atlas_0.png` and horse `single_atlas_1.png`; its `material-map.json` maps exact native LOD1 names, and root's pinning expands same-role LOD2/3 only after native atlas identity verification. Stock preview positions a rider low near the mount; the same pose appears in stock projection and is not native match evidence.

Exact Sultanate combat elephant III/IV/IV-upgrade donors have editable compatible atlas blends and maps under `art/exports/release_060/remaining_roster/nonhumanoid`. Their native geometry, 140-bone animation and equipment are retained. The cloth/armor color treatment is modest at game size; no source-only additions are represented as compiled silhouette changes. The worker elephant is a distinct Tughluq 24-group/8-palette/140-bone model: `tug_worker/v8-tug24-native-names` exports the approved riderless open crate and three sticks only in basket groups 0–2, with all 21 conditional cargo/body groups retained. `v9-native-names-snapshot/snapshot.json` proves its authored geometry/skeleton equals v7 except object naming and pins v8 FBX. **The fresh native v8 cook/readback failed identity**: 24 source groups/eight palettes became 18 native groups/one palette, with merged left/right cargo and group-name drift (`output/release_060/authored-geometry/tug-worker-v8/comparison.json`). This geometry is unqualified and must not be integrated as a compiled worker. Earlier basket albedo remains a material-only option; its appearance does not include the crate.

The exact reached English siege tower skin, English trebuchet siege cart skin, and the separately attached recurve bow, Chinese Monk III staff, basic spear I/II, and Chinese ji spear III/IV charge/brace models have compatible material maps and editable blends. The siege cart preview shows a complete stock carriage.

Spear III/IV normal melee uses native tassle/billhook variants, while charge and brace use `chi_spear_ji`; the ji material map applies to those reached leaves only. Other tool/crew attachments require the binding audit before claiming appearance coverage.
