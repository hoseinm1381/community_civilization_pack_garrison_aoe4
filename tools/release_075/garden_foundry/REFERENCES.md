# Regional reference boundaries

Read 2026-10-23. These support materials and functions, not an exact reconstruction.

- [Thang Long archaeological site](https://hoangthanhthanglong.com/di-tich/di-tich-khao-co-hoc/?lang=en): Ly–Tran brick courts, stone column foundations, timber-column buildings and ceramic roof ornament. The Garden retains a fictional compact waterside pavilion composition.
- [Thang Long lotus lake, Vietnamese government cultural information](https://scov.gov.vn/dat-nuoc-con-nguoi/hoang-thanh-thang-long/ho-sen-dau-tich-ho-co-trong-khu-di-tich-18-hoang-dieu-tai-di-san-van-hoa-the-gioi-hoang-thanh-thang-long.html): archaeological lake evidence and royal water/garden systems support retaining the pond as the composition's centre. Current plantings and small border arrangement are artistic interpretation, not a documented medieval planting plan.
- [Co Loa heritage centre, bronze casting moulds](https://thanhcoloa.vn/en/discovering-the): casting furnaces, air-feed pipes, fuel ash, mould fragments, crucibles and slags support purposeful fuel and material organization. Co Loa evidence is substantially earlier than the retained cannon works; it does not authenticate the precise chimney or cannon layout.
- [Vietnam National Museum of History, Luy Lau ceramic mould study](https://baotanglichsu.vn/DataFiles/2020/10/News/thong%20bao%20khoa%20hoc/Truong%20Dac%20Chien%20-%20Ve%20nhung%20manh%20khuon%20duc%20trong....pdf): ceramic bronze-casting mould discoveries support a ceramic/stone casting language. Again, the cannon-era workshop is an interpretation, not a copied archaeological plan.

Official AoE4 principal-body counts and full-compound comparison remain separate root-owned evidence. A principal body triangle count is used only as a conservative authoring ceiling, never represented as a refreshed complete simultaneously visible compound measurement.
